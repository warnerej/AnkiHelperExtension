# AnkiHelper Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-22_

AnkiHelper is an Anki add-on that shares your study stats with groups you join. Nothing is uploaded until you agree in the consent dialog.

## What we collect
- Email address and username (account).
- Per day, for the last 365 days: number of reviews, retention, and minutes studied.
- Group membership (which groups you own or joined).

We never collect card contents, deck names, or review times.

## Who can see it
- Members of any group you join can see your username and your daily stats (up to 365 days, including history from before you joined).
- Your email is never shown to other users.

## Where it is stored
Data is stored with our hosting providers: Supabase (database and authentication) and Render (API server).

## Retention and deletion
Data is kept until you delete your account. Use Tools > AnkiHelper > Delete account in Anki (`DELETE /auth/me`) to remove your account and stats permanently. Owned groups pass to the longest-standing member, or are deleted if empty. Turning off Tools > AnkiHelper > Sharing stops new uploads but does not delete data already stored.
