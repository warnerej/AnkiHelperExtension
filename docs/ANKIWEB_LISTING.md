**Title:** AnkiHelper: Shared Study Stats

**Tags:** statistics, heatmap, social, groups

**Description:**

AnkiHelper turns your private Anki stats into a shared progress view. It draws a GitHub-style year heatmap of your reviews on the deck browser, and lets you study alongside friends in groups.

**Features**
- Year heatmap of your daily reviews, with daily average, days learned, and current and longest streak.
- Groups: create one or join with an invite code, then see a group-total heatmap and one heatmap per member.
- Retention and study minutes per day, alongside review counts.
- Your past year is uploaded once, and reviews done on your phone are picked up after each sync.

**Privacy: sharing is opt-in**
Nothing is uploaded until you accept a consent dialog. Once you do, AnkiHelper uploads your email, username, and per day (last 365 days) your review count, retention and minutes studied. Members of groups you join can see your username and those daily stats. Card contents, deck names and review times are never uploaded. Stop any time from Tools > AnkiHelper > Sharing, and remove your data with Tools > AnkiHelper > Delete account.

**Getting started**
1. Install, restart Anki, then open Tools > AnkiHelper.
2. Sign up or log in, and accept (or decline) sharing.
3. Open the Groups tab on the deck browser to create or join a group.

**Notes**
- Requires Anki 26.5 or newer.
- The server runs on a free tier, so the first login after a quiet period can take up to a minute.
